# Xtivio IPTV for Samsung Smart TVs

A free IPTV player for Samsung TVs. No ads, no restrictions, and it runs on older Tizen models too.

Xtivio plays what you give it. It provides no channels or streams of its own.

## Features

- Playlists: M3U / M3U8 (URL or file), Xtream Codes and Stalker / MAG portals
- Live TV with a programme guide, catch-up where the provider supports it
- Movies and series with resume
- Favourites, categories you can hide and reorder, parental PIN
- Pair from your phone: add a playlist by entering a short code on the TV, no typing with the remote
- 23 interface languages
- Optional account sync between your devices

## Install

Xtivio is installed on the TV through Apps2Samsung (see its instructions for your TV).

1. Follow the Apps2Samsung setup for your TV.
2. Choose Xtivio from its list.

You can also download the `.wgt` from [Releases](../../releases) and check the SHA-256 shown in the release notes against the file. A `.wgt` needs to be signed with your own Samsung certificate to install on a TV, which is what Apps2Samsung does for you.
