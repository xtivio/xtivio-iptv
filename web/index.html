Settings

Performance Mode
Lite reduces work on slower devices: smaller data chunks with more pauses, fewer images at once, a 1-day guide, and no background preloading of movies/series. Auto picks for you based on the device. Choose Lite if the app feels sluggish, or Normal to keep everything at full detail.
Language
Changes the language of the app's own text — menus, buttons and messages. Channel, movie and series names come from your provider and are not translated. The app will reload when you change this.
Text Size
Scale everything in the app up or down for easier reading. Affects channel lists, the guide, settings, and the on-screen keyboard. Video playback is not affected.
Screen Margin
Pulls the whole app in from the edges of the screen. Use it if your screen cuts off the edges of the picture. Off uses the full screen.
Playback Buffer
Extra video kept ready ahead of what you are watching, to ride out short network or provider dropouts. Standard starts fastest and keeps live channels closest to live. A larger buffer uses more memory and live channels may play a little behind live. What you actually get depends on your device memory and your provider.
Preferred Player
Auto: native on devices (hardware decoder, supports HEVC + most live streams); hls.js on desktop browsers. Native: always uses the device/browser's built-in player. HLS Only: forces hls.js (rarely useful on a device — won't play HEVC).
Theme
Choose a dark or light appearance, or follow the device setting. Video playback always stays dark.
Touch / Remote Mode
Some devices misreport whether they have a touchscreen, which can turn on phone-style gestures and layout on a device with no touchscreen at all (or the reverse). If the app doesn't match how you're actually controlling this device, override it here. Auto uses the device's own reported capability. The app will reload when you change this.
Video Scaling
How the video fits the screen. Contain shows full picture, Cover zooms/crops, Stretch Height targets cinema letterbox, Stretch Width targets classic 4:3 pillarbox.
Movies & Series View
List shows a category list with title rows. Cards shows a poster grid with a category dropdown.
Live TV View
List shows the guide-integrated channel rows. Cards shows a channel logo grid with a mini player and now/next; the full guide stays in List.
Info Overlay Duration
How long the channel and programme info bar stays on screen after you change channels. Choose Always to keep it visible.
Clock on Player
Show the time in the corner of the picture while watching. Off keeps the clock in the info overlay only.
Match Frame Rate
Switch the TV to the refresh rate of the video (for example 24, 25 or 50 Hz) so motion is smooth. Seamless only changes when the TV can do it without a blank screen. Always may blank the screen for a second when a stream starts. Needs a TV or box that supports it.
Resume Last Channel
Automatically start playing the last watched channel when the app opens.
Cache Movies & Series
Keep the Movies and Series catalogue on this device so the app opens without re-downloading it every time. It refreshes automatically after 30 days, when you change the playlist's source, or when you refresh the playlist manually. Turn off if you prefer a fresh download on every launch.
Cloud Sync
Sign in to sync your sources, favorites, watch history, category order, hidden & locked categories, and subtitle preferences across all your devices. Free and optional.
Sync Actions
Zero-Knowledge Credential Sync
Off by default, provider passwords are never synced. Turn this on to sync them end-to-end encrypted with a passphrase only you know. The passphrase is never sent to the server and cannot be recovered if lost.
